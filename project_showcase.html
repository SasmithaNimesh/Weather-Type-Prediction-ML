Phase 1 • Exploratory Data Analysis

Understanding Patterns in Weather Categories

We analyzed distributions and variable relationships across 11 input features to identify the primary drivers behind weather conditions.

Key Meteorological Discoveries

🌡️

Temperature & Freezing Threshold

Snowy conditions are strictly segregated by sub-zero temperatures (mean -4.2°C). Sunny days concentrate heavily above 25°C, while Cloudy and Rainy occupy the intermediate 10°C–24°C envelope.

📉

Barometric Pressure Collapse

Atmospheric pressure dropped significantly during Rainy events (mean 995 hPa) compared to clear Sunny days (mean 1018 hPa), aligning with cyclonic low-pressure weather systems.

🌧️

Precipitation & Humidity Interaction

Rainy and Snowy classes exhibited high relative humidity (>80%) and precipitation probabilities (>70%), whereas Sunny days rarely exceeded 45% humidity.

☀️

Solar Radiation & UV Index

UV Index peaked at 7–14 for Sunny classifications, while cloudy/rainy days averaged UV indices below 3 due to thick cloud filtration.

Phase 2 • Feature Engineering & Selection

Data Preprocessing & Domain Features

How data cleaning, domain-specific meteorological modeling, and mutual information ranking amplified model predictive signal.

1. Meteorological Feature Derivations

Raw measurements were synthesized into meteorologically proven compound indexes:

Dew Point Approximation
T_dew = Temperature - ((100 - Humidity) / 5)
Temperature-Humidity Index (THI)
THI = 0.8 * T + (RH/100) * (T - 14.4) + 46.4
Barometric Pressure Anomaly
Delta_P = Atmospheric_Pressure - 1013.25 hPa
Sub-Zero Freezing Indicator
Is_Freezing = 1 if Temperature <= 0 else 0

Phase 4 • Practical Decision-Making

Supporting Real-World Operational Decisions

Machine learning predictions are paired with operational logic to trigger automatic mitigation and safety protocols across critical industries.

✈️
Aviation & Flight Safety
  • Low-Visibility IFR: Automatically switches from Visual (VFR) to Instrument Flight Rules when visibility drops below 3.0 km.
  • Crosswind Advisory: Warns flight control for ground speeds > 30 km/h.
  • Airframe De-Icing: Enforces runway & aircraft de-icing whenever Snowy weather or sub-zero freezing rain is detected.
🌾
Smart Agriculture
  • Irrigation Conservation: Pauses automated center-pivot sprinklers when rainfall probability > 60%, preventing water waste & root rot.
  • Frost Protection: Triggers thermal crop covers and orchard heaters when temperatures fall below 0°C.
  • Heat Stress: Recommends increased hydration scheduling when UV index is severe and temperatures exceed 32°C.
🚚
Logistics & Fleet Transit
  • Highway Speed Reduction: Mandates 20% speed reductions and triples vehicle following distance during rain or snow.
  • Tipping Hazard Warning: Restricts high-profile freight trailers on exposed highway bridges when wind speeds exceed 35 km/h.
  • Route Optimization: Reroutes time-critical supply chains away from low-pressure storm corridors.
🎪
Outdoor Public Safety
  • Severe UV Protection: Enforces shaded rest tents and hydration stations at public gatherings when UV Index ≥ 7.
  • Event Venue Relocation: Prompts event organizers to activate indoor contingency locations ahead of precipitation.
  • Cold Stress Warnings: Restricts extended outdoor public exposure in freezing blizzard scenarios.
